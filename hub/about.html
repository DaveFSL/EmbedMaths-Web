About & privacy

What EmbedMaths is

Daily practice for written methods, place value and measurement. Work it out on paper, then check each step.

Who made it

Made by Dave Cox, a Year 6 teacher in Brisbane (FlowState Learning).

Privacy

No accounts, no names, no tracking. Progress is saved only on the device being used, and clearing the browser clears it. Nothing is sent anywhere.
